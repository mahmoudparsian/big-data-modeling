# mapreduce_with_pyspark

Two complementary write-ups on MapReduce implemented in PySpark: a
comprehensive intro/tutorial, and a larger catalog of worked examples.

|---|---|---|

| [`mapreduce_with_pyspark_examples.md`](mapreduce_with_pyspark_examples.md) | md | *"MapReduce for Big Data: Examples by PySpark"* — simple, intermediate, and complex MapReduce-style problems solved directly in PySpark. |
